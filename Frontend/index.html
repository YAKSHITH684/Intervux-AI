Intervux is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed for students, fresh graduates, and professionals who want to build confidence before real interviews. It brings together intelligent resume analysis, personalized AI mock interviews, and performance tracking into a single, easy-to-use platform.

Users can upload their resume to receive ATS scoring, skill extraction, and actionable improvement suggestions. Based on their resume, target role, and experience level, Intervux generates personalized HR, technical, and behavioral interview questions that closely simulate real interview scenarios.

After every interview session, Intervux evaluates responses, provides detailed feedback, highlights strengths and weaknesses, and recommends areas for improvement. A personalized dashboard tracks interview readiness, resume quality, skill development, and overall progress over time.
